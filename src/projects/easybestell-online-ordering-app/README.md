# Easybestell (Online Ordering App for Clients)

Online food ordering application for restaurant customers.

- **Period:** 2023 – 2024
- **Relationship:** built at MIROTECH GROUP

## Overview

The customer-facing ordering app of the Easybestell restaurant ordering and operations platform, alongside the admin panel and the restaurant management app. Five frontend developers worked across the ecosystem, with different developers owning different areas. Orders placed here flow into restaurant operations, where the platform's automatic ticket printing (a local Electron printing bridge; I contributed to the architecture and frontend integration) gets kitchen tickets printed without browser dialogs.

## Responsibilities & Contributions

- Frontend development on the customer-facing ordering app within the shared Easybestell platform
- Contributed to the automatic ticket-printing architecture and its frontend integration
