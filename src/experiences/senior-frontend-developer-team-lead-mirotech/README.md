- **Location:** Remote
- **Period:** 2022-04 – 2025-12

- Oversaw frontend development across the company's full product line, including Tadilo (B2C travel booking), Terminal101 (B2B booking), MiroPay, Booking ERP, Easybestell, and Dassfund
